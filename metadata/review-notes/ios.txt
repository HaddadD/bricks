2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
BRICKS! is a single-player block puzzle for all ages: drop pastel pieces on a 10x10 board and clear full rows and columns. It's for anyone who wants a calm, quick game for a few spare minutes.
The problem it solves: most casual puzzle games interrupt play with ads, timers, sign-ups and pay-to-continue prompts. BRICKS! has none of these: it opens straight into a game, is free with no purchases, and works offline.
The value: a relaxing, skill-based puzzle you can pick up anywhere. Games carry on where you left off and follow you between your Apple devices, and Game Center adds achievements and leaderboards for players who want goals.

3. HOW TO USE IT (no login, no setup, no sample files)
- Open the app; a game starts straight away.
- Drag a piece from the tray below the board onto it. A row or column that fills up clears and scores. The game ends when no tray piece fits.
- Power-ups (bar under the tray): Hammer (smash one brick) and Bomb (clear a 3x3 area): tap one, then tap the board. Shuffle deals a new tray, Rotate turns the tray pieces, Undo takes back a move. A gift on the board pays out a power-up if you build over it and clear its row or column before its countdown ends.
- Gear icon (top right): Dark mode, Vibrations, Float pieces (iPhone: lifts a dragged piece above your finger), Widget on/off, Reset game.
- Trophy icon (when signed in to Game Center): achievements and leaderboards.
- Also included: a Home Screen widget (also shown in StandBy and CarPlay; can be turned off in Settings) and an Apple Watch app (tap a piece, tap the board to aim, tap again to place).
- iPad: landscape and resizable windows. iPhone Duo: the outer display folds the power-ups into the side column.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
All are Apple's; there are no third-party services, SDKs, analytics, ads or AI.
- Game Center (optional): achievements, leaderboards, challenges and the Games app.
- iCloud key-value storage: the game in progress and best score, so you can continue on another device signed in to the same iCloud account. Works offline without it.
- The public App Store lookup (itunes.apple.com/lookup): checks for a newer App Store version. Nothing about the player is sent.
No account, sign-in, server, user-generated content or in-app purchase.

5. REGIONS
The app and its content are the same in every region.
