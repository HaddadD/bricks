Not needed for the Mac version unless requested; the iPhone version's App Review Information has one. The app has no account registration, login, account deletion, user-generated content or paid content.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
BRICKS! is a single-player block puzzle for all ages: drop pastel pieces on a 10x10 board and clear full rows and columns. It's for anyone who wants a calm, quick game to fill a few minutes, with no ads, accounts, timers or purchases. Games carry on where you left off and follow you between your Apple devices.

3. HOW TO USE IT (no login, no setup, no sample files)
- Open the app; a game starts straight away.
- Drag a piece from the tray beside the board onto it with the mouse or trackpad. Full rows and columns clear and score. The game ends when no piece fits.
- Power-ups: Hammer and Bomb (click one, then click the board), Shuffle, Rotate and Undo.
- Menu bar: New Game (Cmd-N), Undo (Cmd-Z), Settings (Cmd-,): Dark mode and Reset game.
- Trophy icon (when signed in to Game Center): achievements and leaderboards.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
All are Apple's; there are no third-party services, SDKs, analytics, ads or AI.
- Game Center (optional): achievements, leaderboards, challenges and the Games app.
- iCloud key-value storage: the game in progress and best score, so you can continue on another device signed in to the same iCloud account. Works offline without it.
- The public App Store lookup (itunes.apple.com/lookup): checks for a newer App Store version. Nothing about the player is sent.
No account, sign-in, server, user-generated content or in-app purchase.

5. REGIONS
The app and its content are the same in every region.
